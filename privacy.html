Privacy Policy

YTW · Last updated 14 November 2026

Who this applies to

YTW is a personal tool with a single user: its developer, who runs it on their own computer and connects only their own YouTube channels. It has no public sign-up and no other users. This policy describes what YTW does with the data it can reach through a connected Google Account.

YouTube API Services

YTW uses YouTube API Services to work with connected YouTube channels. By using YTW with a YouTube channel, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What YTW accesses

With the permission granted on Google's consent screen, YTW can:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the connected channel, and read back the status of those uploads;
  • read the connected channel's basic details: its name, handle, picture, country, and subscriber, view and video counts;
  • read YouTube Analytics reports for videos on the connected channel, such as views, impressions, click-through rate and watch time.

YTW does not read the account's email, contacts, files, or any Google service other than YouTube.

How the data is used

  • Access tokens are used to carry out the uploads and reads listed above, and for nothing else.
  • Channel details and analytics figures are shown to the user in YTW's own dashboard and are used to judge how published videos performed, so later videos can be planned better.

Where the data is kept

Everything YTW obtains through a connected account stays on the user's own computer: the OAuth tokens in a private file, and the channel details and analytics figures in a local database. YTW has no server of its own and does not transmit this data anywhere else.

Sharing

YTW does not sell, rent, or share data obtained through a connected Google Account with any third party, and does not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the user deletes it. The user can remove a connected channel inside YTW, which deletes the stored token for that channel, and can delete the local database at any time.

Access can also be withdrawn on Google's side at any time from the Google Account permissions page. After that, YTW can no longer reach the channel.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the project's issue tracker.
