YTW

A personal, single-user tool for preparing and publishing videos to the operator's own YouTube channels.

What it is

YTW runs on one computer that belongs to its developer. It helps that one person research topics, write scripts, assemble videos and, after a human has reviewed and approved each video, upload it to a YouTube channel that the same person owns.

YTW is not a public service. Nobody else can sign up for it or sign in to it, and it is not offered for sale.

How it uses a Google Account

When the developer connects one of their own YouTube channels, YTW asks Google for permission to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions to that channel;
  • read the channel's basic details, such as its name and its subscriber, view and video counts;
  • read YouTube Analytics reports for the videos on that channel.

YTW uses this access only for those purposes. The details are in the privacy policy.

YouTube API Services

YTW uses YouTube API Services. Use of YTW with a YouTube channel is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
